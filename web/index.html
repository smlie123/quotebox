Why QuoteBox?

Perfect for saving long articles, fleeting ideas, or important images. Images are stored locally, so even if the original link dies, your content remains safe.

⚡️

Instant Rich Capture

Select text or images, right-click, and "Save to QuoteBox". It preserves the original formatting, links, and styles.

✏️

Edit & Refine

Not happy with the saved format? Use the built-in editor to tweak styles, remove clutter, or add your own notes.

�

Flexible Viewing

Browse your collection your way: switch between Waterfall, Card, or List view to find the perfect layout for your needs.

📂

Stay Organized

Categorize your quotes, search through your collection, and add annotations to keep your thoughts structured.

🔒

Private by Design

Your data lives in your browser (IndexedDB). No tracking, no cloud servers, no accounts required.

📤

Data Ownership

Export your entire library to JSON format anytime. You own your data, always.

How it Works

Build your personal knowledge library in three simple steps.

1

Select

Highlight any text or right-click an image on any webpage you're reading.

2

Save

Choose "Save to QuoteBox" from the context menu. You'll get a subtle confirmation.

3

Review

Open the extension to view, organize, edit, or export your saved collection.
