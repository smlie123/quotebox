About Quotebox

Quotebox is a Chrome extension designed to help you capture meaningful content while browsing the web.

With a simple selection and right-click, you can save text or images instantly — without interrupting your reading flow.

Why Quotebox?

The web is full of valuable ideas, but most of them disappear once a tab is closed.

Quotebox helps you turn those fleeting moments into a personal, searchable knowledge library — built gradually, one quote at a time.

Local & Private by Design

Your data is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB.

No accounts No tracking No cloud storage

You fully own and control your data at all times.

What You Can Do

  • Save selected text or images from any webpage
  • Organize content with categories
  • Search, edit, and annotate your collection
  • Export and back up your data anytime

Built for Thinkers

Quotebox is built for readers, writers, researchers, and lifelong learners — anyone who values ideas and wants to keep them close.

Export Data

Export all your data (categories and articles) to a .json file.

To restore: go to Settings → Import Data, then select the exported file.
